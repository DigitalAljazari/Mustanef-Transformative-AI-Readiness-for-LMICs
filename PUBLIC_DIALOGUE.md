## Approach

Mustanef’s public-dialogue approach is designed to:

- translate complex AI developments into accessible Arabic and English;
- connect technological change with lived social and cultural questions;
- include educators, writers, researchers, women, youth, and community voices;
- identify public concerns that should inform policy and research;
- strengthen informed participation rather than passive technology adoption.

Public learning and community engagement are developed through **ANAwAI**, Mustanef’s applied public-facing stream.
