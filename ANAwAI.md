# ANAwAI — Ana wa AI | أنا والذكاء الاصطناعي

**ANAwAI** is Mustanef’s accessible public-learning and community-engagement stream. It is not a separate competing initiative; it translates Mustanef’s research, policy, and foresight questions into forms that people and communities can understand, discuss, and apply.

## Purpose

ANAwAI helps individuals and communities examine how artificial intelligence may affect their learning, work, health, relationships, rights, opportunities, and future choices.

## Proposed Activities

- Accessible bilingual learning materials
- Public seminars and facilitated dialogues
- Community workshops and awareness sessions
- Short courses and digital learning resources
- Youth, educator, and civil-society engagement
- Anonymized pre- and post-activity learning assessments
- Community feedback feeding into Mustanef research and policy work

## Guiding Principles

- **Accessibility:** explain AI without unnecessary technical barriers.
- **Inclusion:** engage people who are often excluded from technology debates.
- **Human agency:** help communities shape AI futures rather than merely adapt to them.
- **Accountability:** address risks, rights, fairness, and responsible use.
- **Local relevance:** connect global AI developments with LMIC realities.

## Evidence Base

ANAwAI builds on Digital Al-Jazari’s documented public AI activities since 2025. Planned expansion remains subject to partnerships and resources.
