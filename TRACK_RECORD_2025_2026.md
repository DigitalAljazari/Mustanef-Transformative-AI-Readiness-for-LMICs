# Track Record: 2025–2026

This record documents completed public, cultural, educational, and institutional activities that form the early foundation of Mustanef. It does not present future milestones as completed achievements.

## 2025 — Jordanian Writers Association

Digital Al-Jazari organized an event at the Jordanian Writers Association combining recognition of pioneering Jordanian women with a seminar on artificial intelligence. The seminar was introduced by **Prof. Dr. Ahmad Madi**.

- Evidence: [Recorded event](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eDXn8S9n3w&t=4850s)
- Significance: Demonstrates that Digital Al-Jazari’s public AI engagement was active in 2025 and connected technological discussion with cultural and social leadership.

## 27 June 2026 — Senior Pioneers Forum

The forum hosted the seminar **“Artificial Intelligence and Its Relationship to Life”**, with the participation of **Dr. Dalal Anabtawi** and presentation by writer **Maram Rahmoun**.

- Evidence: [Recorded seminar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uY7jlSVP9z0&t=3968s)
- Significance: Public dialogue examining the relationship between AI and everyday life, supported by documented media coverage.

## 2026 — Book Friends Club

Digital Al-Jazari presented a public dialogue asking what might happen if artificial intelligence became transformative and changed work, health, wellbeing, and even marriage and social relationships. The seminar was presented by **Ms. Asia Al-Ansari**.

- Evidence: [Recorded dialogue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iT2dka9B5ic&t=4s)
- Significance: Direct evidence of Mustanef’s transformative-AI public-dialogue track.

## 2026 — Second Scientific Day

Digital Al-Jazari participated in the Second Scientific Day of the Directorate of Education and Military Culture / Military Culture Schools. The event addressed **artificial intelligence in education and digital transformation toward future-ready education**.

- Evidence status: Photographs supplied; exact captions and credits are pending verification before public upload.
- Significance: Institutional engagement connecting AI readiness with education and digital transformation.

## Supporting Digital Capacity

Digital Al-Jazari maintains academic journals and the **Skills & Certificates** training platform, which currently contains approximately 300 courses. These platforms support future bilingual knowledge dissemination, education, and public engagement under Mustanef and ANAwAI.
