# Strategic Roadmap: 2026–2030

This roadmap presents strategic directions and proposed milestones. Except where linked to documented evidence, it should not be read as a record of completed activities. Implementation depends on partnerships, funding, and institutional cooperation.

## 2026 — Foundation and Evidence Consolidation

- Consolidate the 2025–2026 public-engagement record.
- Establish the public GitHub knowledge repository.
- Define the Mustanef and ANAwAI relationship and bilingual identity.
- Organize media, video, institutional, and photographic evidence.
- Develop initial partnership and funding materials.

## 2027 — Readiness Framework Development

- Develop a Jordan-focused transformative-AI readiness assessment.
- Map implications for education, health, work, social protection, governance, and inequality.
- Conduct stakeholder consultations and public dialogues.
- Produce initial policy briefs and accessible ANAwAI learning resources.

## 2028 — Testing and Comparative Learning

- Test and refine readiness tools with relevant stakeholders.
- Compare Jordanian findings with selected LMIC contexts.
- Expand bilingual public-learning activities.
- Document lessons, limitations, and locally adaptable practices.

## 2029 — Partnership and Regional Adaptation

- Build regional research and civil-society partnerships.
- Adapt the framework for additional LMIC settings.
- Develop thematic guidance for priority sectors.
- Strengthen knowledge exchange between researchers, policymakers, and communities.

## 2030 — Open Knowledge and Long-Term Preparedness

- Publish an updated open readiness framework and supporting tools.
- Consolidate comparative lessons and policy recommendations.
- Establish a longer-term network for transformative-AI preparedness.
- Define the next strategic phase based on evidence and technological developments.

## Cross-Cutting Commitments

- Inclusion and social justice
- Responsible and accountable AI
- Local capacity and knowledge ownership
- Bilingual accessibility
- Evidence-based policy
- Community participation
- Adaptability to rapidly changing AI capabilities
