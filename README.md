# Mustanef — Transformative AI Readiness for LMICs

**Mustanef** is a strategic initiative of [Digital Al-Jazari for AI & Research Innovation](https://digitalaljazari.org/) working to strengthen readiness for transformative artificial intelligence in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs).

Mustanef brings together research, policy, strategic foresight, public dialogue, partnerships, and future preparedness. It examines how rapidly advancing AI may reshape education, health and wellbeing, employment, economies, social protection, governance, inequality, and human development—and how LMICs can respond with local capacity, inclusion, accountability, and long-term resilience.

## Why “Mustanef”?

The Arabic word **مستأنف** carries the sense of beginning anew, resuming with renewed purpose, and reconsidering a matter in light of new realities. The name reflects the initiative’s central proposition: countries should not merely adopt AI tools; they should reassess and redesign their preparedness for an AI-transformed future.

## Initiative Structure

- **Mustanef:** research, policy, foresight, readiness frameworks, partnerships, and strategic dialogue.
- **ANAwAI (Ana wa AI / أنا والذكاء الاصطناعي):** Mustanef’s accessible public-learning and community-engagement stream.

## Core Areas

- Transformative-AI scenarios and strategic foresight
- AI readiness in low- and middle-income countries
- Education and future skills
- Health, wellbeing, and social protection
- Employment and economic transition
- Governance, inclusion, accountability, and inequality
- Public understanding and community dialogue
- Cross-sector and international partnerships
